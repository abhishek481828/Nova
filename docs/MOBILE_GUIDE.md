# Nova Mobile v3.0 — User & Operational Guide

## Overview
Nova Mobile v3.0 is an offline-capable, privacy-first AI companion for Android. It operates as a standalone assistant on your device while seamlessly syncing with your Nova Core laptop when connected to the same network.

---

## Getting Started

### Installation
1. Install `NovaMobile-v3.0.0-release.apk` on Android (Android 8.0+ supported, Android 12+ recommended).
2. Launch **Nova Mobile**.
3. Grant **Microphone** and **Notification** permissions when prompted.
4. Verify the continuous foreground service status notification: `"Nova Assistant is active"`.

---

## Core Features & Voice Commands

### 1. Wake Word Detection
- Say **"Hey Nova"** to activate immediate speech capture.
- Nova glows visually on screen and plays a subtle audio feedback tone.

### 2. Local Commands
- **Calls & Messages**: `"Call Mom"`, `"Send SMS to Alex saying I will be late"`
- **Device Controls**: `"Turn on flashlight"`, `"Set brightness to 70%"`, `"Set volume to 50%"`
- **App Launching**: `"Open YouTube"`, `"Open Maps"`, `"Open Camera"`
- **Alarms & Timers**: `"Set alarm for 7:00 AM"`, `"Set timer for 10 minutes"`

### 3. Built-in AI Skills
- **Calculator**: `"Calculate 125 times 8"`
- **Unit Converter**: `"Convert 10 km to miles"`, `"Convert 25 celsius to fahrenheit"`
- **Translator**: `"How do you say hello in Japanese?"`
- **Device Status**: `"What is my battery level?"`
- **Notes**: `"Take a note: Buy groceries tonight"`

### 4. Smart Automations & Routines
- Say `"List my routines"` to view all active routines.
- Say `"Pause all automations"` for instant safety pause.
- Say `"Enable driving routine"` to activate specific automations.

### 5. Multi-Device Sync
- When your laptop running **Nova Core** comes online on the same Wi-Fi, Nova Mobile automatically pairs and synchronizes your personal preferences, favorite apps, and routine definitions.
